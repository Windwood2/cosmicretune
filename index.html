Close enough to love the world, far enough away to see it whole

Music from the larger view

Resonance Music looks at human life from outside its ordinary frame. It is less about looking up for answers than looking back at us from a wider horizon and hearing what finally becomes obvious.

Resonance Music is not defined by how it sounds. It is defined by what happens when you hear it.

Resonance Music

Songs from just outside the frame

These songs begin where ordinary explanations start to thin out. Identity, belief, sovereignty, memory, humor, fear and possibility are viewed from a little farther away, then brought back into the intimacy of a human voice.

The sound may move through soul, progressive rock, spoken word, blues, psychedelia or something harder to name. The continuity is the point of view.

Core idea

Recognition rather than instruction

The songs do not try to prove metaphysics or preach an answer. They circle an idea until something in the listener recognizes itself inside it.
