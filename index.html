Music from the larger view

Resonance Music looks at human life from outside its ordinary frame. It is less about looking up for answers than looking back at us from a wider horizon and hearing what finally becomes obvious.

Resonance Music is not defined by how it sounds. It is defined by what happens when you hear it.

What this site is for

A simple home for the music

This version is intentionally simple. Add an MP3, paste lyrics into a plain text file, optionally drop in a video, and the page is ready. No hand-editing a hundred files. No line-break codes. No fuss.

Core idea

Recognition rather than instruction

The songs do not try to prove metaphysics or preach an answer. They circle an idea until something in the listener recognizes itself inside it.

Pages included

What you get in this simpler version

Home page, song library, one reusable song page template, philosophy page, about page, visuals page, starter artwork, and a plain-English README. Everything runs from a small set of files so the site stays manageable.

Add a song
  1. Open js/songs.js and duplicate a song block
  2. Change the title, slug, and short description
  3. Drop your MP3 into media/audio using that slug as the filename
  4. Paste lyrics into data/lyrics/your-slug.txt
  5. Refresh the browser
