# ROOM WITHIN A ROOM

[Verse 1]

A shape rose quietly around us
before we had a name.
One wall gathered into another.
A doorway learned its frame.
Weather settled on the windows.
Water entered wood and stone.
Something closed without a sound—

and we mistook the room for home.

We learned morning from the window,
learned tomorrow from the clock.
Every footstep gained a meaning.
Every question found a lock.

[Pre-Chorus]

Count the corners if you must.
Trace the edges of the room.
If this place contains the whole world,
why does one thin seam still hum?

[Chorus]

Something here remembers
what we no longer know.
Something moves beneath the surface
where no measured distance goes.

We named the light and shadow.
We named the wind and flame.
Built a world from what surrounded us—

then entered our own names.

Still—

beneath the noise of living,
something waits without a name.

[Instrumental Turning]

[Verse 2]

Years collected in the timber.
Dust settled into grain.
We repaired what time had broken,
then watched it break again.

We filled the shelves with answers.
We marked the ceiling, mapped the floor.
Every answer widened something.

Every answer made one more.

Why can music feel remembered
before the melody has begun?
Why can somewhere never entered
feel more familiar than where we’ve come?

Why does longing lean toward something
that no compass ever knew?
Why does darkness recognize us
before we’re passing through?

[Pre-Chorus]

Leave the edges where they are.
Let the silence answer slow.
Maybe what we called a boundary
was only somewhere we wouldn’t go.

[Chorus]

Something here remembers
what we no longer know.
Something moves beneath the surface
where no measured distance goes.

We named the light and shadow.
We named the wind and flame.
Built a world from what surrounded us—

then entered our own names.

Still—

beneath the noise of living,
something waits without a name.

[Bridge]

Who put the handle on the doorway?

Who decided it was closed?

Who stood beyond the farther side
before a farther side arose?

Was silence hiding something—

or waiting to be heard?

Was distance ever distance
before we gave it word?

We searched above the ceiling.
We searched beneath the floor.

Then someone asked the smaller question:

Who are we

before the door?

[Instrumental Ascent]

[Drums gather into an uneven heartbeat]

[Verse 3]

No thunder split the building.
No voice came through the air.
Nothing reached across to tell us
what had always waited there.

Only something in the question
changed the place from which we saw.

The room remained exactly where it was—

but no longer held us all.

The footsteps beyond the doorway
had always matched our own.
The stranger moving through the silence
had never been unknown.

We had furnished every distance.
We had painted every sky.
Made a home from our forgetting—

so completely we could ask why.

[Quiet Refrain]

Ask again.

Not what waits beyond it.

Not what lies inside.

Ask what kind of traveler
could leave itself behind.

Ask again.

Not where the doorway opens.

Not which side is true.

Ask who stands on either side—

and hears the asking too.

[Final Chorus]

Now the colors keep their stories.
Now the currents keep their names.
Nothing here must be abandoned.
Nothing there must stay the same.

We can remain beside the window.
We can hear the distant sea.
We can cross without departing.

We can stay
and still be free.

And the doorway—

still exactly where we left it—

no longer tells us where we are.

[Instrumental Release]

[Analog tone passes through them and continues alone]

[Outro]

The footsteps beyond the doorway
had always matched our own.

One room opened through another—

and neither stood alone.

We crossed with nothing in our hands
but what we had become.

Both rooms remained behind us.

Both rooms brought us home.

[Fade Out]
[End]
