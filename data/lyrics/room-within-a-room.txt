# ROOM WITHIN A ROOM

Who are we---

before the door?
[Verse 1]


Form rose up quietly around us
before we ever had a name.
One wall gathered up another.
A doorway learned its frame.
Weather settled on the windows.
Waters washed wood and stone.
A door shut in the distance—

and we mistook this room—[slow breath]
                                  for home.

We learned Of morning from the window,
Found tomorrow in the clock.
Every footstep taught us meaning.
Each question stacked another block.

[Pre-Chorus]

Count the corners if you must.
Trace the edges of the floor.
If this place is now the whole world,
why does that wall have—

[Brief silence]
[voice shouts]

                     another door?

[Chorus]


Something here remembers
what we no longer know.
Something moves beneath the floorboards
where no measured distance goes.

We named each light and each shadow.
We named the wind and then the flame.
Built a world from what surrounds us—
[Pause]
Then entered— [voice shouts]
                               our own game.

Still—

Beneath the noisy ness of living,
Silence waits without a name.

[Verse 2]

Years collected in the timber.
Dust settled in the grain.
We repaired what time had broken,
then watched it break again.


We questioned every changing shadow,
every current, every flame.
Measured all that moved before us,
gave each mystery a name.
We asked the walls what they were hiding,
asked the distance what it knew.
Never thought to turn the question
toward the one behind the view.

Why can music feel remembered
before the melody has begun?
Why can somewhere never entered
feel more familiar than where we’ve come?

Why does longing lean toward something
that no compass ever knew?
How can darkness recognize us
before we ever come into view?

 [Pre-Chorus]

Leave the edges where they are.
Let the silence answer slow.
Maybe what we called a boundary
was only somewhere we wouldn’t go.

[Chorus]

Something here remembers
what we no longer know.
Something moves beneath the floorboards
where no measured distance goes.

We named each light and each shadow.
We named the wind and the flame.
Built a world from what surrounds us—
[Pause]
Then entered our own game.

Still—

beneath the noise of living,
silence waits without a name.

[Bridge]

Who put the handle on the doorway?

Who decided it was closed?

Who stood beyond the farther side
before a farther side arose?

Was silence hiding something—
or waiting to be heard?

[Pause]

Was distance ever distance
before we made it a word?

We searched above the ceiling.
We searched beneath the floor.

Then someone asked the smaller question:

Who are we---
[pause]

before the door?

Ask again.

What kind of traveler
could leave itself behind?

Ask again.

Does the doorway
Have to separate the rooms?

Now the colors keep their stories.
Now the curtains keep their names.
Nothing here need be abandoned.
Nothing here need stay the same.
We can remain beside the window.
We can hear the distant sea.
We can cross without departing.
We can stay and still be free.

[Outro]

We passed through walls that held us,
We crossed with nothing in our hands
Both worlds stood before us,
Both rooms became—[pause]
                  our infinite expanse.

eeeeha baby!

[End]
