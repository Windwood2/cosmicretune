Belief 1

Trip-hop at 120 BPM with a low, intimate female spoken-rap voice, dry and highly intelligible in the mix; deep sub-bass, sparse kick, dry snare, smoky electric piano, analog synth pulses, distant Hammond, muted guitar, and layered female echoes move from nocturnal tension through fragmented ritual into a spacious, melodic final chorus, ‑trap clichés, ‑glossy pop sheen, ‑generic inspirational anthem, ‑over-singing


[Instrumental Prelude]
[Low pulse, sparse piano, distant voices overlapping like radio fragments]
[Fragments gradually become rhythm rather than intelligible speech]

[Verse 1]

We arrive without a language,
Without a map or name.
Hands already point directions.
Voices tell us what became.

Eat this.
Fear that.
Trust him.
Stay near.

Someone learned to cross the winter,
So the winter became fear.

Every answer had an ancestor.
Every warning had a scar.
Someone lived through something once
And told us what all somethings are.

[Pre-Chorus]

We were children of conclusions
Made before we learned to see.
Someone else's way of living
Slowly hardened into me.

[Chorus]

Born believing,
Raised on borrowed eyes.
Learning how to stay alive
Before we learned to recognize.

Blind hands holding blind hands,
Calling every guess a guide.
We kept passing down the answers
Long after all the answers died.

[Instrumental Turn]
[Drums enter in an uneven marching rhythm]
[Voices begin repeating short phrases underneath]

[Verse 2]

A rumor becomes a warning.
A warning becomes law.
Law becomes a sacred story
No one remembers seeing born.

Tell it often.
Tell it louder.
Paint it red and call it true.
Give a fear enough believers
And it starts believing you.

Soon the body wears the story.
Soon the story runs the blood.
People starve beside abundance,
Fight for names drawn in the mud.

Some will die defending answers
No living person ever knew,
Because a borrowed explanation
Feels more solid than the view.

[Chorus]

Born believing,
Raised on borrowed eyes.
Learning how to stay alive
Before we learned to recognize.

Blind hands holding blind hands,
Calling every guess a guide.
We kept passing down the answers
Long after all the answers died.

[Aside / Breakdown]
[Voices overlap faster]
[Rhythm becomes mechanical]

Everybody knows.
Everybody knows.
Everybody knows because—

Everybody knows.

Read it.
Repeat it.
Share it.
Defend it.

Choose a side.
Choose it fast.

Question that
and lose the tribe.

Question this
and lose the past.

Who said it first?

[Beat]

Nobody knows.

Who checked?

[Beat]

Nobody knows.

Then why—

[Band cuts]

Because.

[Silence]

[Verse 3]
[Single instrument returns]

Maybe “I don't know” is holy
When the old words lose their hold.
Maybe truth arrives uncovered,
Without needing to be told.

Knowing doesn't need an army.
Knowing doesn't need applause.
Knowing doesn't fear a question
Or protect itself with laws.

It can stand beside uncertainty.
It can change when seeing changes.
It can leave an empty space
Where belief once built its cages.

[Bridge]
[Harmony opens, rhythm becomes fluid]

Survival taught us quickly.
Knowing moves another way.
One remembers what was handed.
One can meet what comes today.

Belief says:
Hold the answer.
Knowing says:
Look again.

Belief inherits yesterday.
Knowing enters where we stand.

Maybe wisdom isn't having
One more certainty to keep.

Maybe waking is the moment
We stop dreaming what we repeat.

[Instrumental Expansion]
[The original overlapping voices return, but now spacious and separated]
[Each voice becomes distinguishable before dissolving]

[Final Chorus]

Born believing,
But we don't have to stay.
Borrowed eyes can close behind us.
Seeing starts another way.

Blind hands can release each other
Without leaving anyone behind.
We can honor how we survived here
Without mistaking it for mind.

No final book.
No final teacher.
No last answer carved in stone.

We have lived by what was handed.

Now we're learning
how to know.

[Final Movement]
[Music strips back to pulse and voice]

A child arrives.

The world begins:

Here's what happened.
Here's what matters.
Here's what's dangerous.
Here's who wins.

[Pause]

Maybe someday
we greet that child
with something kinder than belief.

[Long pause]

Look.

Feel.

Question.

Know.

[Outro]
[One unresolved chord]
[The opening radio voices return briefly, then fade before completing their sentences]
