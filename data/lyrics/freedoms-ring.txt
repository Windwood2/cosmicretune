What if we were wrong 

Folk-soul, spacious unhurried 70 BPM sway; strong, clear, grounded mature female lead, conversational close-mic verses and nearly spoken bridge, harmonized choruses with widely spaced backing voices; fingerpicked acoustic guitar, warm piano, subtle Hammond, late-entering melodic bass, restrained brushed percussion; unresolved chord slowly bending, widening drum spaces, guitar notes losing relation, gradually brightening live-band intimacy, tape-warm room detail, deliberately released resolution, ‑rap cadence, ‑trip-hop textures, ‑heavy snare, ‑lush ambient production, ‑country twang, ‑gospel excess, ‑vocal acrobatics, ‑and glossy pop


WERE WE WRONG?

[Low Instrumental Opening]
[One unresolved chord slowly bends out of shape]
[Bass enters late, as though it has been listening]

[Verse 1]

We kept yesterday elastic
Pulled it over everything
Over every contradiction
Till the old shape fit again

Called the tension understanding
Called the recoil what we knew
Every time the world went wider
We stretched yesterday around the new

And it held

Of course it held

That was what we made it for

[Pre-Chorus]

A belief can travel miles
Without ever leaving home

A rubber band remembers
Even while you pull it thin

Let it go

And there you are again

[Chorus]

What if we were wrong?

Wouldn’t that be freedom?

Not defeated
Not forgiven
Not a lesson we survived

What if being wrong
Was simply where the rope came loose?

No one wins

No one loses

Nothing left to prove

[Instrumental Stretch]
[Time loosens; drums remain, but the spaces between them widen]
[A guitar phrase stretches until individual notes almost lose relation]

[Verse 2]

We said release the fear

We understood the sentence

We underlined it twice
And carried it for years

We believed in letting go

We became very accomplished
At believing we could leave behind
The thing we kept rehearsing

Fear learned all our language

It could say surrender too

It knew exactly how to sound
Like something newly true

[Pre-Chorus]

You can stretch a piece of taffy
Till the daylight passes through

Still

Every molecule remembers
What the hands told it to do

[Chorus]

What if we were wrong?

Wouldn’t that be freedom?

Not another better answer
Waiting underneath the first

What if all this certainty
Was only tension holding shape?

And what we called a prison

Was the effort
Not to change

[Bridge — almost spoken]

Here is the part
Nobody wants

Not blame

Not shame

Not punishment

Accountability

Pure enough
To leave nobody else in the room

No teacher

No childhood

No history

No god

No enemy

No version of yourself
Who should have known better

Just—

I made this true

I kept it true

I knew I was keeping it true

And knowing that
Did not make it stop

[Silence]

So now what?

[Second Stretch]

Do something?

We know that move

Push harder
Practice longer
Wake earlier
Watch the mind
Watch the watching
Become the one
Who finally learns
How not to become

Same hand

Different taffy

[Music begins gathering itself again]

Maybe doing
Was yesterday’s word too

Maybe freedom does not answer
To the muscles we have used

Maybe nothing has to happen
At the surface first

Maybe resonance begins
Before the person gets the news

[Final Chorus — restrained, not triumphant]

What if we were wrong?

Wouldn’t that be freedom?

What if truth was never waiting
For our argument to end?

What if fear cannot be conquered
Because conquest keeps it here?

What if something underneath us
Has already disappeared?

[Outro]

I know

I know I know

I have known for years

[Long pause]

But knowing
Still has someone standing there

Holding it

What if we were wrong?
