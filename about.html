About

Orum and the project

A creative field where music, language, visual systems and metaphysical humor meet.

Orum

Orum is a lifelong maker whose work moves through woodworking, CAD/CAM, digital imagery, generative visual environments, songwriting and philosophical inquiry. Resonance Music grows out of that larger artistic life rather than standing apart from it.

The songs are often less interested in conventional genre than in what happens when language and music create recognition in the listener.

Orientation

Resonance Music explores consciousness, identity, sovereignty, memory and the assumptions people inherit without noticing. The project uses AI as instrument, amplifier and collaborator while keeping human direction, taste and selection at the center.
